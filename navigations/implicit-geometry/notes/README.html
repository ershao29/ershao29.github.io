Recommended theme: Verifiable Implicit Geometry and Three-Dimensional Neutron Transport for Complex Deformed Reactor Cores.

Prepared November 10, 2026. This is the English research collection for developing a doctoral proposal. Original Chinese notes, research documents, and demos remain at their original workspace paths. The related Chinese grant-application PDF is excluded at the user's request.

The central question is how a material-aware implicit geometry program can supply reliable interface events and complete characteristic-track segments, while controlling the resulting transport error at an affordable cost.

Start here

  1. Read Research directions for the main argument, mathematical counterexamples, priorities, and novelty boundaries.
  2. Read Technical roadmap for OpenMC/OpenMOC integration, query contracts, validation cases, metrics, and the proposed schedule.
  3. Read Evidence and sources before using external claims in a formal proposal. Its 41 records retain their historical reading/access limitations.
  4. Read Grant-proposal analysis to connect the topic to the existing SDF-MOC application and its unresolved technical issues.
  5. Use the literature map and source-code guide for deeper reading and implementation planning.

The earlier topic discussion provides broader alternatives, including unfitted deterministic methods, response adaptation, sensitivities, and neural representations. sources.json provides machine-readable references; related repositories preserves the existing software survey.

A focused thesis structure

Research component Question Evidence to produce
Reliable multi-material queries Can every relevant interface be located under thin layers, grazing rays, contact, and hierarchy? Stated field assumptions, bounded events, complete material sequences, explicit unresolved/fallback states
3D MOC segmentation and error control How do interface and source-region errors affect optical thickness, volumes, and responses? Independent segment/volume checks, controlled response convergence, geometry-aware adaptation
Local compilation for MC/MOC Can pruning and caching preserve internal semantics and reduce total cost? Equal-accuracy comparisons, build amortization, cache behavior, memory, and failure regimes

OpenMC conservative prefiltering is an early implementation candidate. Random Ray and geometry derivatives are later extensions after the forward geometry is reliable. Targets and schedules in these documents are proposals, not measured achievements.

Reliability and provenance

General implicit fields, exact SDFs, conservative distance bounds, and interpolated zero surfaces have different contracts. A distance lower bound is not a directional boundary hit. Correct endpoint labels do not exclude an intervening thin layer. Material regions and FSRs are distinct partitions. Cross-solver agreement is insufficient if both solvers share the same faulty geometry.

The English package includes full English adaptations of the research-direction/roadmap/evidence documents and condensed English companions for the long literature and grant analyses. Detailed Chinese originals remain available through the original-material index.

Reading questions for the proposal

  • What geometry is authoritative: analytic shape, CAD boundary, triangle mesh, or interpolated field?
  • Which quantities are exact, estimated, or bounded, and over what domain?
  • Can thin layers and every material/source boundary be recovered?
  • Does a claim concern rendering quality, geometry queries, or complete transport responses?
  • Are preprocessing, memory, fallback, and physical errors included in comparisons?

Improved Ray Casting of Procedural Distance Bounds

Paper source. Benjamin Keinert, Henry Schafer, Johann Korndorfer, Urs Ganse, Marc Stamminger. The PDF labels Journal of Graphics Tools 17(4):127–138 with a 2013 issue year, while its cover records online publication July 9, 2015. DOI.

The abstract introduces safe overrelaxation, self-intersection handling, convex-object acceleration, screen-space candidates when tracing fails to converge, and fixed-point treatment of discontinuity artifacts. It concerns procedural distance bounds, so a field can support conservative advancement without being an exact SDF.

For the thesis, inspect the overrelaxation acceptance/fallback conditions and robustness at repeated boundary starts. A screen-space best candidate is a rendering-quality policy; its suitability for physical segment lengths requires separate proof. Preserve the distinction between a safe step and an accurate directional event.

Euclidean Distance Mapping

Paper source. Per-Erik Danielsson, Computer Graphics and Image Processing 14:227–248 (1980).

The paper studies sequential distance propagation in binary images using a two-component descriptor. Its abstract reports small-error Euclidean maps with two scans including forward/backward row movement, and discusses expansion/shrinking and skeletons. The introduction contrasts sequential propagation with iterative parallel updates and city-block/chessboard metrics.

Use it as historical background for discrete Euclidean distance transforms. Pixel-center distance, continuous boundary distance, signed distance, and transport-safe bounds are different targets. The abstract's small-error characterization should not be promoted to a universal exactness theorem for arbitrary 3D geometry.

Walk on Stars

Paper source. Rohan Sawhney, Bailey Miller, Ioannis Gkioulekas, Keenan Crane, ACM TOG 42(4), 2023. DOI.

WoSt extends grid-free Monte Carlo treatment of linear elliptic PDEs to mixed Dirichlet and Neumann conditions using star-shaped domains and reflecting Brownian motion. The abstract identifies visibility-silhouette queries and a normal-augmented BVH as key geometric ingredients.

This explains the Monte Carlo link in Geometry as Code. Useful themes include geometry-query interfaces, progressive pointwise solutions, and complex-boundary handling. Brownian elliptic-PDE sampling does not replace directional, energy-dependent neutron collisions and fission. A diffusion study would require explicit physical assumptions.

Curved-Surface Nodal Framework (CSN)

Paper source, originally ssrn-7300024.pdf. Zhou Xiaoyu, Wang Yizhen, Du Zhengxin, Li Jining, Yin Wen, Xu Ning, Li Fu, Xu Yunlin, Hao Chen. The PDF identifies a non-peer-reviewed preprint. SSRN record.

The abstract separates analytic surface equations defining curved nodal domains from high-order local polynomial solution approximation. It presents a formulation based on a general transport equation and reports neutron, steady/transient thermal, and coupled tests. A helical-cruciform example and comparisons with a fine finite-element reference are author-reported examples, not experiments reproduced during this organization.

This is a high-priority novelty comparison: determine its geometry family, interface representation, integration rules, conservation, neutron discretization, and error/performance baseline. Compare the proposed reliable MC/MOC interface and complete traversal contract against what CSN actually implements. Continuous curved geometry alone should not carry the thesis's novelty claim.

Next reading actions

Read the full methods and error sections before quoting numerical gains. Add a structured evidence record for CSN to a future review rather than silently expanding the historical 41-source index. Distinguish same-geometry query acceleration from changed geometry representation and from changed solution approximation in every comparison.

2. Different strengths of evidence

Claim Evidence Use in this report
Omega's goals and stated development stage S01 author article Author statement, not a performance proof or full current-product audit
UGFs differ from exact distance S02–S05 and mathematical examples here Source concepts, independently computed examples
Useful local pruning/compilation implementations S10–S14 Technical foundation; no transfer of rendering speedup factors to transport
Simple gradient normalization does not ensure safe stepping S13 motivation and independent counterexample The x²−1 example refutes a general claim; no claim to complete another source's theorem
Distance fields have accelerated nuclear tracking S24 conference methods and journal record Avoid “first SDF in nuclear engineering” claims
OpenMC already uses implicit CSG S25–S27 Contributions concern complex fields/reliable queries
OpenMOC supports 3D S30–S32 State mode-specific assumptions; proposed changes are unimplemented
Random Ray is an extension comparison S33–S34 Existing method established; new-backend compatibility unverified
Deformation effects merit research Visible S35–S37 abstracts/methods and bibliographies Do not treat all numerical results of partially accessible papers as checked
Directions, interfaces, schedules, thresholds This report's synthesis Proposals, not achievements in cited literature

4. Use of existing local materials

Existing notes were inspected read-only during the original review to identify background and avoid duplicate topics:

  • Grant-proposal analysis: problem structure and literature leads; key external claims were checked against primary sources.
  • DAGMC-SDF notes: conference/journal distinction; public conference methods were read separately.
  • SDF-grid ray-tracing notes: outline and references only in the original review. The JCGT 2022 webpage had no extractable body and its PDF was not read then due to size limits, so its full text was not treated as a core reviewed source.

The original review did not re-extract every local PDF or the full application, did not report earlier demo results as new measurements, and did not modify existing files. The later workspace organization translates and relocates these materials without changing that historical reading scope.

5. Evidence gaps before formal project approval

  1. Obtain full Luo, Shapiro, and deformation-neutronics texts to check theorem assumptions, references, and reproducible setups. Sampson and Quilez originals were inaccessible in the original review and require legitimate access. The Luo PDF is now available in this collection; the earlier source record remains historical.
  2. Pin OpenMC, OpenMOC, and Fidget releases/commits. Point-in-time develop observations and stable documentation do not establish a validated software combination.
  3. The review covered interfaces/key functions, not full call graphs. Trace OpenMC event insertion/restart serialization and non-extruded compatibility of each OpenMOC OTF mode during implementation.
  4. Continue searches for material-labeled implicit geometry, interval all-intersection tracking, deformation MC/MOC, shape derivatives, and multigroup errors before bounded novelty claims.
  5. S40/S41 are only 2026 further-reading entries; titles/links do not establish mastery or reuse of their algorithms.
  6. Omega descriptions rely on S01; unpublished code, collaboration interfaces, and complete language specifications were not obtained and are not prerequisites.

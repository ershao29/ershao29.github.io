Doctoral Proposal Research Package

Prepared November 10, 2026. Start with the collection's reading guide.

The recommended research theme is verifiable implicit geometry with material and source-region semantics, reliable interface queries and complete 3D characteristic segmentation, followed by geometry-to-transport error control.

Read in this order:

  1. Research directions: source synthesis, field distinctions, counterexamples, six directions, and defensible novelty.
  2. Technical roadmap: OpenMC/OpenMOC integration, query contracts, test ladder, error/performance metrics, and a proposed 24-month schedule.
  3. Evidence and sources: 41 historical source records, access scopes, and remaining evidence gaps.
  4. Grant-proposal analysis: a condensed English analysis of the related SDF-MOC application, prior-art corrections, algorithmic risks, and historical remote-code observations.
  5. Earlier topic discussion: broader MC, MOC, unfitted discretization, sensitivity, and neural-geometry options.
  6. Machine-readable sources: synchronized source metadata in English.

Prioritize reliable multi-material queries, complete 3D MOC/FSR segmentation, and semantics-preserving local compilation. OpenMC conservative prefiltering is an early integration candidate; sensitivities and Random Ray follow forward reliability.

These documents describe research proposals and historical source analysis. They do not report completed OpenMC/OpenMOC transport experiments. Their numerical targets, schedules, and performance expectations are proposed criteria. Existing demo results come from independent teaching programs.

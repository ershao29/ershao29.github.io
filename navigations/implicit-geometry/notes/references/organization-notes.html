Preservation policy

The original workspace documents and demos remain at their original paths, including the Chinese notes and research-proposal package. The English collection is a separate companion under summary. Original PDFs from the workspace are retained there as well as copied into the collection.

The user-provided OpenMC/OpenMOC trees were moved from the top of summary into source-code/openmc-develop and source-code/openmoc-develop. Their internal relative structure and file bytes are preserved. Operating-system metadata is excluded from substantive preservation counts.

Two English PDFs were copied, without changes, from paper-sdf: the Dapogny–Frey distance-computation paper and the JCGT SDF-grid ray-tracing paper. The Chinese grant application was not copied, following the user's explicit instruction.

English documents

Research directions, technical roadmap, earlier topic discussion, and source records have English adaptations retaining their structure and mathematical content. The long literature notes and grant analysis have condensed English companions; the full Chinese originals remain accessible through the original-material index.

Published claims, explanatory derivations, historical code observations, teaching results, and proposed research remain distinct. Historical reading limitations are retained, including in the 41-source index. New brief supporting-paper annotations do not imply a full literature review or add unverified records to that historical index.

ssrn-7300024.pdf was renamed to curved-surface-nodal-pde-framework.pdf after checking its title. Its preprint status remains explicit. The related-repository survey was carried forward as an existing list, without claiming fresh verification of every project.

Demonstrations

The English copies preserve core algorithms, tests, figures, CSVs, and numerical JSON metrics. Only demo documentation, human-readable report text, and report-generation strings were localized. Ray-report image links are now relative, including newly generated reports.

Archived reports retain their original measured data and environment. Their translation does not make them new benchmark runs. The organization checks run separately in temporary output directories and do not replace archived experiments.

Source versions and inventory

The OpenMC archival record identifies commit b4c1d95d42b9217402e40dd7350cb89751223dcf. The supplied OpenMOC tree lacks an equivalent archival commit record. Content digests in collection-inventory.json identify the actual local snapshots; implementation work should pin explicit upstream revisions.

The inventory records final paths, sizes, SHA-256 hashes, and original locations where applicable. Original filenames outside summary are URL-encoded so the inventory remains readable as an English package. Newly authored indexes and summaries are marked separately from unchanged source material.

Verification documents preservation, language, link, and runnable-demo checks. Temporary backup and smoke-run material is outside the collection at ./example-output; the workspace originals are the durable copies.
